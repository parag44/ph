Best Area To Stay

Manila

  • Makati: Greenbelt, cafes, malls, safe city walk.
  • BGC: modern vibe, night walk, clean streets.
  • Airport/MOA: best only for final night before early flight.

Best Area To Stay

Cebu

  • IT Park / Lahug: cafes, Sugbo Mercado, night walk.
  • Cebu Business Park: Ayala Center Cebu and central access.

Skip For This Trip

Keep it city-focused

  • Oslob Whale Shark Watching
  • Kawasan Falls
  • Heavy beach or island activities
  • Too many far places on the same day
  • Simala, unless you really want a long church and architecture day
