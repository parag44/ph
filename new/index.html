Travel Itinerary

Philippines • 8 Days / 7 Nights

A relaxed Manila and Cebu city trip focused on must-visit places, cafes, street walking, photography, heritage spots, malls, food markets, and easy evenings. Cebu stays calm and scenic with no water or adventure activities.

Arrival + Makati / BGC
  • 10:35Arrive Manila Terminal 3
  • 12:30Hotel check-in or luggage drop
  • 15:00Ayala Triangle, Greenbelt, Glorietta
  • 18:30BGC and Bonifacio High Street night walk
  • 20:00Easy dinner and city photography
Day 2
Old Manila + Manila Bay
  • 09:00Fort Santiago and Intramuros streets
  • 10:30Manila Cathedral, San Agustin, Casa Manila
  • 13:00Rizal Park and National Museums
  • 17:00Manila Bay sunset walk
  • 19:00SM Mall of Asia dinner and window shopping
Day 3
Quiapo + Binondo + Divisoria

  • 11:00Binondo and Ongpin food walk

  • 15:00Lucky Chinatown and Divisoria Market
  • 19:00Return to Makati / BGC and pack for Cebu
Day 4
Fly to Cebu + IT Park
  • 08:00Check out and go to Manila airport
  • 10:45Flight from Manila to Cebu
  • 14:00Ayala Center Cebu and Business Park walk
  • 17:30Cebu IT Park cafe break
  • 19:00Sugbo Mercado food walk
Day 5
Cebu Heritage Walk
  • 09:00Magellan's Cross and Santo Nino Basilica

  • 13:00Lunch around downtown Cebu

  • 18:30Carbon Market and Carbon Night Market
Day 6
Sirao + Temple + TOPS
  • 08:30Sirao Garden photo walk
  • 11:00Temple of Leah architecture stop
  • 13:00Busay cafe or viewpoint break
  • 17:00TOPS Cebu sunset and skyline view
  • 20:00Dinner nearby or return to IT Park
Day 7
Mactan Easy Day + Manila
  • 09:00Check out and keep luggage if possible
  • 10:30Mactan Shrine and Lapu-Lapu Monument
  • 12:30Mactan Newtown cafe or souvenir stop
  • 16:00Flight from Cebu to Manila
  • 19:00Dinner near airport / MOA and rest
Day 8
Departure to Dhaka
  • 03:45Flight Manila to Guangzhou, CZ5052
  • 07:00Guangzhou layover
  • 09:35Flight Guangzhou to Dhaka, CZ5015
  • 11:50Arrive in Dhaka
  • 12:30Trip complete

Important Notes

  • Stay around Makati / BGC in Manila and IT Park / Lahug or Cebu Business Park in Cebu.
  • Keep Cebu relaxed: no Oslob, Kawasan Falls, heavy beach, island, or adventure activities.
  • Use Day 7 Option B if you want a safer pre-flight day: Ayala Center, IT Park cafe, souvenir shopping.
